Travel essentials

Travel well.
Travel respectfully.

Simple information for a smoother trip. Entry rules, health guidance, public alerts and local policies can change—always verify them from official sources.

Money

Yen & payments

Japan uses Japanese yen. Cards and contactless payments are common in many places, but cash can still be helpful, especially in small towns.

Connectivity

Maps & translation

Save offline maps, key addresses and translations. Internet coverage can vary in mountain areas, underground spaces and remote islands.

Etiquette

Public behaviour

Queue carefully, keep calls quiet on trains, remove shoes when asked, and avoid tipping unless a venue makes a policy clear.

Weather

Heat, snow & storms

Summer heat, winter snow and typhoon-related disruption all require destination-specific preparation. Follow official alerts.

Safety

Emergencies

Learn local emergency guidance, carry essential medical information, and follow staff instructions in an earthquake or severe-weather situation.

Accessibility

Plan ahead

Many newer stations are accessible, but routes and historic sites differ. Confirm facilities directly when accessibility is essential.

Frequently asked questions

Start here

Can I travel without speaking Japanese?

Many transport signs and visitor sites in major areas include English. Translation tools, saved addresses and patient communication are valuable outside the biggest cities.

Should I get a rail pass?

Compare a current official pass’s terms with your exact itinerary. Eligibility, coverage and fare rules can change, so use official information before buying anything.

Where do I check entry requirements?

Consult Japan’s official diplomatic and immigration sources for your nationality, and check your own government’s travel advice.
