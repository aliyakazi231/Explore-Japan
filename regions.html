Travel planning

Regional differences matter.

Climate

Northern and mountain areas have serious snow seasons, while the southwest is subtropical. Check the destination—not just the country—when packing.

Access

Frequent urban rail may give way to infrequent buses, ferries or driving in rural locations. Plan remote transfers first.
